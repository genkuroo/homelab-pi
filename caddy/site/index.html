gen-kuro.com

A handful of small web apps I built and now self-host on a Raspberry Pi 400 at home. Pick one below; every app has an ☰ all apps button in the corner to bring you back here.

Stock Tracker stocks.gen-kuro.com demo data
A portfolio tracker that pulls daily close prices and writes a plain-English weekly recap of what moved, generated by Claude Haiku 4.5. This public copy runs on a synthetic portfolio, not my real holdings.
Fitness Dashboard fitness.gen-kuro.com demo data
Pulls cardio, diet/weight and strength training into one SQLite database and lines them up on a shared timeline — weekly cross-source joins and Pearson correlations between, say, training volume and bodyweight. Public copy runs on generated data.
D&D Campaign Tracker dnd.gen-kuro.com login required
A multi-user tracker for a D&D 5e campaign: the DM manages the world, players log in to keep their own characters, inventory and journal. Real data — it goes public because it has its own accounts and sessions.
Fantasy Trade Analyzer fantasy.gen-kuro.com password
Grades Sleeper fantasy-football trades twice: the instant market-value verdict, and a retrospective score from how the traded players actually scored in real weekly lineups afterwards. Behind a shared password because it shows other league members' names.
Everything here runs in Docker on one Pi behind an outbound Cloudflare tunnel — no ports are forwarded and the home IP is in no DNS record. Source: github.com/genkuroo/homelab-pi.
